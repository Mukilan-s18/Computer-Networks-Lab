# Experiment 2: Study of Network Cables

## Objective
1. Understand different types of Network cables (UTP, STP, Coaxial, Fiber Optic).
2. Make a cross-wired cable and straight-through cable using a crimping tool.

## Types of Cables
- **UTP (Unshielded Twisted Pair)**: Common in LANs (e.g., Cat5e, Cat6). Easy to install, cheaper, but prone to EMI.
- **STP (Shielded Twisted Pair)**: Shielded to protect from EMI. Used in data centers (e.g., Cat7).
- **Coaxial Cable**: Used for television networks, high bandwidth.
- **Fiber Optic**: Uses light for transmission. High speed, long distance, but expensive.

## Cable Construction
- **Straight-Through Cable**: Both ends follow the same wiring standard (e.g., T568A or T568B). Used to connect different devices (e.g., PC to Switch).
- **Cross-Over Cable**: One end follows T568A and the other T568B. Used to connect similar devices (e.g., PC to PC).

## Steps for Crimping
1. Strip approx 1.5 cm of cable shielding.
2. Untangle and arrange wires based on T568A/B standards.
3. Snip wires to an even level.
4. Insert wires into the RJ45 plug.
5. Use a crimping tool to secure the wires.
